### Contract and Gate Notes

- Requirements are the acceptance contract where design shorthand differs: simple mode counts support/adhesion sub-controls separately; move walls, temperatures, and plate type to Advanced to stay ≤10. Group extra categories under the five specified Advanced headings.
- A quality rung without a shipped compatible process is unavailable. Do not synthesize an untested selectable combination from Standard or compiled defaults; `ladder.ts` must preserve this guard.
- 4a builds gesture/scene structure against an abstract transform adapter. Engine-facing gizmo conversion stays gated until the **4b contract check** pins rotation units, Euler order, and offset origin; 4b completes that integration without changing rollout order.
- Per-object brim/supports remain hidden and settings apply to the whole plate with the pinned API. Follow-up: an engine build exposing per-object configuration or an explicit product decision is required for independent overrides; do not imply support from transform-only arguments.
- No tests, engine downloads, installs, deployments, or code changes are executed by this tasks-artifact phase. Commands below are apply/verification work, not evidence already obtained.
